# Company handbook

## Werkraum Systems GmbH

Werkraum is a fictional Hamburg company that designs industrial energy gateways
and the software used to operate them. Registered-office and tax identifiers in
this demo are deliberately non-valid.

**Mission:** make energy loss visible early enough for factories to act.

**Operating principles:** evidence before opinion; one accountable owner;
customer requirements before promises; small reversible changes; learn from
nonconformity without hiding it.

## Demo users and responsibilities

The people above are fictional process owners. Peter and Krystof are the real
login accounts used to act on their behalf in this showcase. Tasks use
`@peter` for coordination and delivery, and `@krystof` for technical review.
The shared `demo` login is for exploring and editing the example; it is not a
fictional employee. Peter belongs to the Polaris project group, but not the
restricted project or administrator groups.

## Cadence

- Monday: delivery review and Kanban replenishment.
- Wednesday: customer pipeline and requirements review.
- Monthly: objectives, complaints, risks and supplier performance.
- Quarterly: integrated management review.

## Working with Company OS

Put a decision where its work happens. Write tasks as Markdown checkboxes with
`@owners` and `#tags`. Keep customer evidence in the customer or project folder.
Use artifacts for views and workflows, not as a second source of truth.
