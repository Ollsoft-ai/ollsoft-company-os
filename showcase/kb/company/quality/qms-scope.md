# Quality management system scope

**Organization:** Werkraum Systems GmbH (fictional)

**Scope:** design, configuration, delivery and support of industrial
energy-monitoring gateways and their hosted management software from the Hamburg
office, including outsourced enclosure manufacturing and field installation.

## Interested parties

| Party | Relevant need | How it is monitored |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial customers | Accurate data, secure integration, predictable delivery | Requirements review, acceptance evidence, satisfaction checks |
| Employees | Clear priorities, safe work, useful knowledge | Weekly review, retrospectives, onboarding feedback |
| Suppliers | Stable specifications and forecasts | Supplier scorecards and change notices |
| Authorities | Tax, product, privacy and employment obligations | Legal register and specialist review |
| Owners | Sustainable growth and controlled risk | Management cockpit and quarterly review |

## Boundaries and exclusions

The demo does not claim certification. Product calibration is performed by an
approved external laboratory and remains controlled as an externally provided
process. No requirement is declared non-applicable merely for convenience.
