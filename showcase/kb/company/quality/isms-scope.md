# Information security management system scope

The illustrative ISMS covers hosted telemetry, customer configuration, employee
endpoints, Company OS, approved SaaS suppliers and the people and processes that
operate them. Factory control systems owned by customers are outside Werkraum's
control, while interfaces and responsibilities at that boundary remain in scope.

## Security objectives

- Preserve confidentiality of customer site and credential data.
- Protect integrity of readings, configuration and acceptance evidence.
- Maintain availability agreed in customer contracts.
- Assess security obligations before commercial commitment.

## Evidence set

Asset ownership, access decisions, supplier reviews, incidents, risk treatments,
training and internal audits are retained with their owner and review date.
