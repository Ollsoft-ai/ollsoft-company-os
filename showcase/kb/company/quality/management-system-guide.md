# Quality and security: follow the evidence

The demo scenario is dated **6 September 2026**. The examples show how a team
could organize a management system; no certification or external audit is implied.

## A concrete example

The customer expects a conforming enclosure. Batch B-441 failed inspection.

1. [Customer requirements](../sales/rheinwerk-handover.md) define acceptance.
2. [Decision D-017](../../projects/polaris-energy-gateway/decision-log.md)
   holds the defective batch and records the release condition.
3. [Corrective action CA-2026-014](corrective-actions.md) records containment,
   cause, action and effectiveness review.
4. [Risk R-021](risk-register.md) addresses dependency on one enclosure supplier.
5. [Delivery Kanban](../dashboards/delivery-kanban.html) holds the assigned work.
6. [Audit programme](internal-audit-programme.md) schedules a check of the evidence.

ISO 9001 does not prescribe a sales funnel. The demo's commercial stages implement
Werkraum's own review procedure. A checkbox records an assertion; its evidence
and responsible reviewer still matter.

The objectives page contains explicitly labelled illustrative baselines. The
cockpit counts actual indexed tasks; it does not calculate conformity or certification.

Reference editions are recorded for this scenario. Check the applicable editions
when adopting the templates. Official reference pages:
[ISO 9001 climate amendment](https://www.iso.org/standard/88431.html) and
[ISO 19011 auditing guidance](https://www.iso.org/standard/19011).
