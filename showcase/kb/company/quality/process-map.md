# Integrated process map

## Leadership

`Context → policy → objectives → resources → management review`

## Customer value stream

`Lead → qualification → requirements → review → proposal → order → design/configure → verify → deliver → support → feedback`

## Enabling processes

- People and competence
- Supplier control
- Information security and access
- Documented information
- Infrastructure and monitoring
- Finance and statutory records

## Improvement loop

Plan objectives and controls, do the work, check evidence and performance, then
act on findings, complaints, risks and opportunities. Every process has an
owner, intended output, measures, risks and retained evidence.

| Process | Owner | Measure | Current target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer requirements | Mina | Reviews complete before proposal | 100% |
| Product delivery | Jonas | On-time accepted installations | ≥ 95% |
| Corrective action | David | Actions closed by due date | ≥ 90% |
| Security risk | Sara | High risks with funded treatment | 100% |
| Customer support | Jonas | Median first response | < 4 business hours |
