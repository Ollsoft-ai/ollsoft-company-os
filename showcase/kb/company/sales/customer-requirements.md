# Customer requirements and opportunity review

There is no ISO-defined sales funnel. Werkraum uses a commercial workflow whose
gates support ISO 9001 customer communication and requirements controls.

## Gate model

1. **New lead** — contact, source, business problem and lawful follow-up basis.
2. **Qualified** — fit, decision process, approximate value and next action.
3. **Requirements review** — stated and unstated needs, delivery constraints,
   statutory requirements, security/privacy needs and acceptance criteria.
4. **Proposal** — feasibility, deviations, price, capacity and approvals reviewed.
5. **Won / handover** — accepted requirements, owners, dates and evidence handed
   to delivery without relying on salesperson memory.
6. **Lost** — reason captured for analysis and improvement.

investigation, response, closure and learning workflow aligned to ISO 10002:2018.
